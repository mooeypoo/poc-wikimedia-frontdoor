# Use wiki content

Access articles from Wikipedia, media files, structured data, and more with public APIs and downloads.

## Introduction to Wikimedia

Wikimedia content is organized by project and language. Each project hosts a different type of free content, like encyclopedia articles on Wikipedia, media files on Wikimedia Commons, and structured data on Wikidata.

[**Explore Wikimedia projects**](https://www.wikimedia.org/)

Most Wikimedia projects are created and maintained in a single language. What we think of as Wikipedia is actually over 300 different Wikipedias—English Wikipedia, Cebuano Wikipedia, Swedish Wikipedia, and many more—all writing original articles in their own language. In contrast, a few Wikimedia projects are maintained in English and translated into other languages (like Wikimedia Commons), or they are created to be language neutral (like Wikispecies).

[**Find projects in your language**](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:SiteMatrix)

## Licensing and attribution

Most Wikimedia content is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA), which means you are free to share and remix the content as long as you provide attribution, link to the license, and indicate any changes. Some projects, like Wikimedia Commons, license pieces of content individually, so it’s important to check the content page for the applicable license.

### Download content in bulk

Wikimedia content is also available to download in bulk, giving you access to all pages from a given project. Bulk downloads are available in HTML and source formats.

[**Learn more about bulk downloads**](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:MyLanguage/Data_dumps)

### Commercial and high-volume access

Wikimedia Enterprise offers APIs to download bulk snapshots of Wikimedia content at scale.

[**Learn more about Wikimedia Enterprise**](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:MyLanguage/Data_dumps)
