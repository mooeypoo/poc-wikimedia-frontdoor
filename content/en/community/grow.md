# Learn and share technical skills

Grow your skills, or help others learn through workshops, internships, and mentoring opportunities.

## Outreach and mentoring programs

Grow your skills through internships and mentoring programs like Outreachy and Google Summer of Code.

[Read more on mediawiki.org](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/Special:MyLanguage/Outreach_programs)

## Videos and tech talks

Learn about Wikimedia technology through recorded presentations from the community.

[Visit the YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/user/watchmediawiki/videos)

## Tech ambassadors

Volunteer your skills to help Wikimedians resolve technical issues and connect developers with local wikis.

[Read more on Meta-Wiki](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:MyLanguage/Tech/Ambassadors)
